# Open Engineering Text

Engineering knowledge, written as reusable text.

![Open Engineering Text hero-banner.png](../assets/hero-banner.png)

Open Engineering Text is the textual knowledge layer of Open Engineering.

We create, curate, structure, and continuously improve texts that help people and AI systems understand, build, explain, and evolve engineering systems.

Element-Oriented Engineering automates the Envelope, preserves the Letter, and continuously grows the Library.

⸻

📚 The Library

Open Engineering Text treats engineering knowledge as a living library.

Rather than keeping knowledge trapped in isolated documents, we make it:

* structured — organized around identifiable engineering elements
* versioned — maintained alongside the systems it describes
* reusable — designed to be referenced, remixed, and composed
* machine-readable — suitable for tooling, agents, and automation
* human-readable — useful to engineers, architects, makers, and learners
* continuously evolving — improved as the engineering system evolves

The result is a growing body of engineering knowledge that can be used by both humans and machines.

⸻

✉️ Envelope, Letter, Library

Open Engineering Text follows a simple distinction:

Envelope

The Envelope describes the structure around a piece of engineering knowledge:

* metadata
* identity
* relationships
* conventions
* classification
* provenance
* machine-readable context

Letter

The Letter is the actual knowledge:

* explanations
* decisions
* methods
* specifications
* tutorials
* architectural reasoning
* engineering principles
* research
* stories and examples

Library

The Library is the continuously growing collection of Envelopes and Letters.

Together they make engineering knowledge discoverable, composable, and reusable.

⸻

🧩 Element-Oriented Engineering

Open Engineering Text is part of Element-Oriented Engineering (EOE).

EOE treats engineering elements as first-class objects that can be described, related, parsed, validated, composed, and evolved.

Text therefore becomes more than documentation.

It becomes an engineering element.

A text can be:

* discovered by an agent
* referenced by another element
* validated against conventions
* transformed into another representation
* connected to architecture
* used as input to a composer
* incorporated into a learning path
* continuously improved through engineering feedback

⸻

🤖 Text for Humans and AI

Modern engineering increasingly involves collaboration between humans and AI.

Open Engineering Text provides a shared knowledge surface for that collaboration.

Humans can read the texts.

AI agents can retrieve and reason over them.

Engineering tools can parse and transform them.

Architectural models can reference them.

Repositories can evolve alongside them.

This creates a feedback loop:

Engineering → Text → AI → Engineering

⸻

🏗️ Part of Open Engineering

Open Engineering Text is one part of the broader Open Engineering ecosystem.

It connects naturally with:

* Open Engineering Architecture — models the systems and their relationships
* Open Engineering Definitions — defines engineering elements and their semantics
* Open Engineering Conventions — establishes consistent engineering practices
* Open Engineering Parsers — turns structured knowledge into usable representations
* Open Engineering Rules — validates engineering knowledge and structure
* Open Engineering Capsules — packages reusable engineering knowledge
* Open Engineering Composers — combines elements into larger systems
* Open Engineering Sites — presents engineering knowledge to people
* Open Engineering Academy — turns knowledge into learning experiences
* Local LLMs Management — provides local intelligence for working with the Library

The Text layer gives these elements something essential:

meaning.

📝 What You Will Find Here

The Open Engineering Text ecosystem can contain many forms of engineering knowledge:

Engineering

Specifications, concepts, principles, patterns, methods, and technical explanations.

Architecture

Architectural decisions, system descriptions, models, and relationships between engineering elements.

Research

Research notes, technology evaluations, experiments, and observations.

Conventions

Repository conventions, naming conventions, metadata conventions, and ways of working.

Learning

Courses, tutorials, examples, explanations, and learning paths.

Stories

Narratives that make complex engineering ideas understandable and memorable.

Memos

Small, focused pieces of knowledge that can be independently discovered, discussed, implemented, and evolved.

⸻

🗂️ memo.md

A particularly important building block is the humble:

memo.md

A memo captures a small, actionable piece of engineering knowledge.

It can record:

* an idea
* a technology evaluation
* an architectural observation
* a proposed convention
* an implementation direction
* a research finding
* an integration opportunity
* an experiment
* a decision

Memos make the Library incremental.

Knowledge does not need to be perfect before it becomes useful.

It can start as a memo and evolve into a specification, convention, implementation, course, architecture, or reusable engineering element.
